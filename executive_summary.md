# Delivery reliability and customer experience

**Olist marketplace, Brazil · September 2016–October 2018**

Delivery reliability is part of the post-purchase experience. Olist's order history points to where operations can investigate missed promises and where product can test better customer updates.

## Five findings

1. **8.11% missed the stored estimate.** Of 99,441 orders, 96,478 were delivered, 625 canceled, and 609 unavailable. Among 96,470 eligible deliveries, 7,826 arrived late. Delivery took 12.56 days on average. Late orders missed by 9.55 days on average.
2. **Late orders received lower ratings.** Among reviewed deliveries, **54.07%** of late orders scored 1–2 stars versus **9.22%** on time. Mean scores were **2.57/5** and **4.29/5**, respectively.
3. **Slower handoff coincided with more misses.** Late rates were **12.94%** for carrier handoff over three days after approval (30,318 orders) and **5.97%** within three days (64,764 orders).
4. **49 sellers exceeded the marketplace late rate in both 2017 and 2018.** Each met minimums of 100 eligible orders overall and 25 per year. Seller `1ca7077d` posted **17.31%** late in 2017 and **26.79%** in 2018.
5. **Rate and volume point to different priorities.** Maranhão had **19.67%** late and **3.83/5** versus Paraná at **5.00%** and **4.24/5**. Rio de Janeiro recorded **1,664** misses. Among named categories with 500+ orders, Electronics led by rate (**9.81%**) and Bed, Bath & Table led by count (**811**).

## Recommended actions and success metrics

| Priority and evidence | Operational implication | Proposed action | KPI to monitor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Slow handoff** · 12.94% vs. 5.97% late | Teams can flag risk during fulfilment. | Pilot a queue for active orders awaiting handoff three days after approval. | Share awaiting handoff at day three and late rate among flagged deliveries. |
| **2. Recurring misses** · 49 sellers | Repeat misses warrant targeted review. | Sample pickup, packing and inventory exceptions at high-volume flagged sellers. | Late rate and late count by seller. |
| **3. Concentrated misses** · 1,664 in Rio de Janeiro and 811 in Bed, Bath & Table | High counts help prioritize investigations. | Audit routes into Rio de Janeiro and fulfilment for Bed, Bath & Table. | Late count, late rate and mean late days by cohort. |
| **4. Poor ratings** · 54.07% vs. 9.22% | A missed promise is a useful trigger for testing better communication. | Test a proactive revised ETA against a holdout for orders at risk of missing the estimate. | 1–2-star share and review response rate by group. |

Route, order mix and promise length can affect these observational comparisons. Using calendar dates yields a **6.77%** late rate. Confirm the promise convention before setting a production KPI. [Dashboard](../outputs/charts/delivery_dashboard.png) · [Methodology](../README.md#methodology).

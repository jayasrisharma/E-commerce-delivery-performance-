# E-commerce Delivery Performance & Customer Experience Analysis

## Version A — Myntra Product Management

- Analyzed 99,441 Olist orders to diagnose delivery reliability and customer experience. Found an 8.1% late-delivery rate, with 1–2-star reviews on 54.1% of reviewed late orders versus 9.2% on time.
- Identified 49 sellers with above-benchmark late rates in two periods and proposed a carrier-handoff alert, seller reviews, and KPIs to track delivery and rating outcomes.

## Version B — General Analyst

- Built a Python, Pandas, and SQL pipeline across seven Olist tables, validating joins and reconciling seven SQL result sets across 99,441 orders.
- Analyzed fulfilment by seller, category and state. Found a 12.9% late rate for handoffs over three days versus 6.0% within three days and visualized priorities in Matplotlib.
